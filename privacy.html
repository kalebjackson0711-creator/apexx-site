1. Information We Collect

Contact, account, and business data

When you purchase, inquire, submit the Get Started form, or onboard, we may collect your name, business name, email address, phone number, mailing or service address, industry details, city, website URL, preferred contact method, what you need (receptionist, website, or both), notes you provide, and other information you voluntarily provide. Onboarding may include hours of operation, service menus, FAQs, scripts, offers, booking rules, routing preferences, brand assets (logos, copy, imagery), and phone numbers you authorize us to use.

Payment data

Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. (“Stripe”). We receive confirmation of payment status, receipt identifiers, plan selection, and limited billing metadata needed for fulfillment and support. We do not store full payment card numbers (PAN), CVV, or full bank account numbers on our servers. Card data is handled by Stripe under Stripe’s own terms and privacy practices.

AI receptionist, call, and message metadata

To operate Frontline, we and our voice/AI infrastructure providers may process call and messaging metadata such as timestamps, duration, caller ID (when available), call outcomes, booking or lead capture results, and configuration logs. Call or message content may be processed as needed to answer customers, follow your playbook, book appointments, capture leads, and improve reliability. You are responsible for complying with recording-notice and consent laws applicable to your business and callers.

Website and device data

When you visit our sites, we may automatically collect standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring URL, pages viewed, and approximate location derived from IP. We may use cookies or similar technologies for basic site function, security, and analytics.

SMS and text-message consent records

If you opt in to text messages on a form (for example the Missed-Call Revenue Calculator), we store your mobile number, the exact consent language you agreed to (including version), checkbox state, submission timestamp, IP address, user agent when available, and the page URL. We use this record to prove consent for A2P 10DLC and TCPA compliance and to honor STOP/HELP requests. We do not send marketing texts without that affirmative opt-in, and texting remains paused until our carrier registration is approved.

  • To deliver, configure, host, and support the Services you purchase, including Frontline and Apex Forge websites.
  • To process lead inquiries from forms (including Get Started, Leak Check, Consent, and the Missed-Call Revenue Calculator) and follow up by email, phone, or text when you have opted in.

  • To communicate about onboarding, fulfillment timelines, account issues, and service updates.
  • To improve quality, security, performance, and customer experience of our systems.
  • To comply with law, enforce our Terms, and protect rights, safety, and property.
